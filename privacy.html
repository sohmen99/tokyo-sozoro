TOKYO SOZORO — Privacy & Support

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

TOKYO SOZORO respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

TOKYO SOZORO does not collect, store, sell, or use personal information for advertising or tracking.

The app may access your device's location in order to determine nearby destinations, calculate direction and distance, and provide the walking experience.

Location information used by TOKYO SOZORO is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers.

TOKYO SOZORO does not require users to create an account.

Analytics and Advertising

TOKYO SOZORO does not use personal information for advertising or user tracking.

Open Data

The app uses public and open datasets to provide destination information and estimated crowd patterns.

Crowd information is an estimate and does not represent guaranteed or real-time occupancy.

External Services

Some features may open external map services or websites. Information handled by those external services is subject to their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change.
